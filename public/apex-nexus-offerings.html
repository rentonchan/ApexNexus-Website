Offering 02

Smart Receipt & Expense Capture

Snap a receipt, drop it in a folder, and it becomes a checked, tax-ready entry in your books, with the original filed by tax year.

How it works

  1. Capture: photo, scan or PDF into one Google Drive folder, straight from your phone.
  2. Read: AI picks out date, vendor, amount, currency and payment method, in Chinese or English.
  3. Record: a new line in your ledger (Notion or Google Sheets), receipt attached, category suggested.
  4. File: the original is renamed and moved to the right tax-year folder, as your 7-year backup.
  5. Review: about 5 minutes a week to check and confirm.

What you get

  • HK tax-ready: by Year of Assessment (1 Apr–31 Mar), with deductible and capital items shown separately.
  • Smart checks: flags meals with no business purpose, unclear totals and likely personal items.
  • Foreign currency converted to HKD at the day's rate.
  • Weekly autopilot, or on demand before a deadline.
Best for: sole proprietors, freelancers, consultants, small teams
Typical launch: 1–2 weeks
